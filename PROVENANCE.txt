## Corrected interpretation of the patch
Because driverID == eAmdProprietary on this host, the stock guard
  (architecture == AMD_GCN) && (driver_id != eAmdProprietary)
evaluated FALSE, so the GCN MMQ warptile tuning was SKIPPED by stock. The
patch's second hunk REMOVES that exclusion and therefore ENABLES the tuning
on this machine. Both hunks are operative; see FAQ_CORRECTION.md.
